Frequently Asked Questions

This course is designed to offer value to students both beginner and more advanced. This may seem a contradiction, but we will be fully honest to say that students without experience may find themselves having to work significantly more time and effort into the course and biology. However, often it is not prior experience but rather how hard a student works (as evidenced by our 3 freshman finalists in 2021). The course does not favor one over the other. The USABO runs an Open Exam around February and a Semifinal Exam around March, and both dates shift from year to year. The syllabus covers the same body of material that both exams draw on.

We think that anyone who has enough dedication/commitment to the course will be able to succeed. Generally it is not the students who have the most experience of the most advanced biology courses that do the best but rather those that put in the most effort and dedicate their time will succeed. That being said, completing at least Honors Biology is recommended.

While live participation is highly participated and beneficial, we totally understand that it can be hard to make it to all the sessions, every single class is recorded and made available within a week to those who miss class. We have also introduced a new flexible, university-style office hours which will be flexible and give students opportunity for live help even if they cannot make sessions normally!

Our course provides most of the materials a student may need to succeed in the course with the exception of textbooks. The only text you definitely need is Campbell’s Biology. Any edition 10th or later will suffice. If you need to buy an older edition 9th and 7th are also very good.

For each week we recommend you do the Suggested Readings for that week before the main lecture. More advanced students may want to peruse our additional readings after each weeks lecture.

The booster classes are small, test-prep oriented sessions that focus on review of basic material and old tests. They are offered usually as “intensives” and are some of the more popular course offerings.
