Course Overview

Baology runs a 40-week year in two 20-week semesters. Semester 1 (starting May) and Semester 2 (starting September) are designed to teach complementary material. However, they are run independently and students may take either of the semesters. Joining after a semester has begun is possible, but the fee and refund deadline are unchanged, so the earlier you join, the more of the 120+ hours of material you can attend live.

  • Semester 2 runs Sept 19 to Feb 5
  • 20 weeks, 5 to 7 classes a week by level
  • 6 to 8+ hours a week, by level
  • 120+ hours of live class at Level 1 or Level 2, 160+ with the Full Package (about $12.50 an hour, or less)
  • Custom weekly Problem Sets with Live Review
  • All instructors are Past IBO competitors/Campers from Ivy+ institutes
  • See our full syllabus
  • Watch our info session
  • Try a free week before you enroll
