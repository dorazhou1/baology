Course Description

The premier USA Biology Olympiad (USABO) course, taught by competitively successful Ivy+ instructors. Baology prepares students for biology competitions through weekly lectures by Harvard graduate Dr. Kevin Bao and his talented staff. Many office hours are available each week teaching skill building, problem solving, new concepts and more. Click here to learn more!

Why Baology?

Since 2021, Baology students have earned 52 USA Biology Olympiad (USABO) National Finalist places and 12 spots on Team USA at the International Biology Olympiad. That is 43% of every National Finalist place awarded since 2021, and 50% of every Team USA place.

Our flagship course offers 20 weeks of live class, with 6-8+ hours a week. Level 1 is for students new to biology competitions, and Level 2 goes deeper. They are $1500 each, or $2000 for the Full Package with both, in total about $12.50 per hour of live class, or less. Every class is recorded, and a full week is free to watch before you pay anything. Course Structure

Baology follows a regular cadence of six sessions during the week (Lecture Primer, New Concepts, Skill Building, Problem Solving, Case Study, and Homework Review) complementing the Main Lecture each Saturday. Custom weekly problem sets with live review keep students sharp, and all classes are recorded so you can watch and review at any time. We also give assigned readings each week to be completed before the main lecture and suggested readings for students curious to learn more.
